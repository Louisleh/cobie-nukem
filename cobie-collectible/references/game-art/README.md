# Cover-reference hierarchy for collectible V2

These images are art-direction inputs, not orthographic identity evidence.
They may guide silhouette, pose, costume, prop language, and palette, but they
cannot approve Cobie's real-world likeness. The owner photo pack and a human
identity review remain open.

## Binding hierarchy

1. **Primary geometry and pose canon:** `assets/brand/cobie_nukem_cover.png`
   (`1003×1568`, SHA-256
   `d9261853c1c5013b22164667d24958e639fcc6e97967e342be017f63a1810d6f`).
   Preserve the single Fetch Launcher, closed/confident mouth, wide planted
   stance, broad paws, aviators, collar/tag, and black biker jacket.
2. **Secondary surface and hard-surface reference:**
   `cobie_nukem_dual_blaster_cover_reference.png` (`1024×1536`, SHA-256
   `70d17b427cd8e4c6a686b34cf188155bf180d5de7e9e16f83556e5f497d18478`).
   Use its apricot fur layering, leather construction, metal response,
   sunglass finish, chunky weapon detailing, and orange/gunmetal/tennis-ball
   palette.
3. **Engineering baseline:** the neutral packet at
   `validation-renders/prototype-v1/`, tagged
   `collectible-prototype-v1-baseline`.

## Explicit conflict decisions

- Do not copy the secondary cover's second blaster.
- Do not copy its open mouth or tongue.
- Do not copy background enemies, signage, title treatment, or layout into the
  physical figure.
- The primary cover's Fetch Launcher remains the one hero prop.
- Rendered strands and micro-scratches may inform look development, but
  printable detail must be modeled as robust clumps, folds, panels, or relief.

## Provenance

The primary cover is the project-original OpenAI-generated image already
recorded in `docs/ASSET_MANIFEST.md`. The secondary cover was supplied directly
by the owner in the Codex task on 2026-07-29 as a project reference. It is
retained unchanged; no external download or third-party asset was used.
