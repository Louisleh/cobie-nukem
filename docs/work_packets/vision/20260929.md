## V1 — Dependable play from launch to retry

Problem: real package entry/firing stalls and incomplete sustained reset/heap evidence undermine every art improvement. Decompose into a bounded browser observer, WebGL-call attribution, first-document and repeated-entry measurements, comparable same-state reset/heap samples, then a measured repair of the dominant cost. Preserve source/package hashes, observer bounds, console evidence and rejected observations. Do not count loading-screen, death-screen or paused time as live combat; do not equate rAF or JS heap with GPU frames or whole-engine memory. Warm caches and instrumentation overhead must be labeled. No recurring allocation growth, teardown errors, trapped Back/pause/retry, or hidden work during active combat accepted. Gates remain existing native/Web budgets; proposed new thresholds need a baseline and explicit rationale. Map: WCB-001–004, WCB-008 startup/performance.

## V2 — Rain City has a signature world

Problem: sparse geometry and uneven material/landmark hierarchy still undersell Cobie. Decompose into an in-engine visual target, one complete critical-route neighborhood, warm/cool hierarchy and authored material families, readable scale/proportions, original environmental jokes, then the remaining accepted Rain City route. First refinement: attached cornice, convincing oven silhouette and differentiated service bays. Assets retain editable source, deterministic export, provenance, platform budgets and independent rendered review. Require actual moving gameplay plus four aspects, collision/nav preservation, performance and console checks; promotional art never substitutes for runtime. Gate: coherent human-reviewed route, not a mesh-count target. Map: WCB-008, ART_BIBLE.

## V3 — Combat has Cobie's swagger

Problem: functioning weapons/enemies do not establish a satisfying first-person rhythm. Decompose existing three-weapon kit into anticipation/action/recovery timing, tactile viewmodel animation, hit/kill/shield response, restrained readable VFX, distinct sound/transient hierarchy, and existing enemy tell/counterplay readability. Prioritize a frequent weapon/enemy pairing before applying to the kit. No new weapons, enemy variants or reward systems. Use bounded ownership packets, matched live traces and animation captures, pooled effects and existing input/accessibility contracts. Gate: consistent readable feedback and human feel/fairness/mix approval with clean budgets. Map: WCB-006/008 and existing combat contract.

## V4 — Rain City is a mission worth remembering

Problem: deterministic routes and boss phases do not prove authored pacing or spectacle. Audit the existing route for exploration/pressure/release rhythm, spatial loops, revisit meaning, observation-led secrets, environmental counterplay and escalating encounter compositions. Refine only an explicitly transferred existing encounter first. Existing Towmaster finale receives a separate later ownership packet for telegraph/silhouette/arena/escalation/defeat payoff, after prerequisites; this brief does not reopen frozen boss work. Gate: complete 15–22-minute mission route meeting PRD spatial/encounter/finale gates, with repeatable checkpoint recovery and human pacing/humor/fairness approval. Map: WCB-005–008.

## V5 — An opening that makes players want more

Problem: title and Salmon Creek pilots are improvements, not a proven hook. Shape the first 30 seconds, then first 3–5 minutes around confident disobedience of the sports-field rule, immediate agency, one clearly taught combat choice, spatial invitation and a memorable small payoff. Deliver the same authored quality as the accepted Rain City target without increasing content breadth. Preserve progression/collision/navigation, validate first entry and death/retry, and inspect continuous opening play rather than isolated stills. Gate: an unfamiliar player can begin, understand the immediate fantasy, and choose to continue without an explanation. Human comprehension, humor and comfort remain open. Map: WCB-008J/K. Can progress alongside V2/V3 under narrow ownership.

## V6 — One definitive slice, proven in real play

Integrate V1–V5 into one source-bound candidate. Prepare full mission and opening review routes, clean sustained native/Web/reset/heap measurements, keyboard/mouse/touch and controller recovery, accessibility settings, audio/motion/flash options, and target device packets. Fix reported failures and rerun the affected checks. Gate: all PRD Rain City mechanical and named human/device requirements receive actual evidence. Automated stills, simulated touch, clean logs or critic scores cannot supply human approval. Map: WCB-009. This is the first milestone that can support removing Rain City's BETA badge, only through the release process.

Preserve shared mechanics; challenge copied presentation that obscures mission identity. Gate: the second mission reaches comparable technical and human quality with measured production cost, proving a repeatable workflow. Other missions stay BETA/frozen until separately scoped. Map: WCB-010.

## V8 — An honest release candidate
